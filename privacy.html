Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Bit Axis Software Solutions (“Bit Axis”, “we”, “us”) collects when you contact us through our website or through our Facebook and Instagram lead forms, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

The short version: we only collect the details you give us so we can reply to your enquiry and talk to you about a project. We don’t sell your information, we don’t use advertising cookies on this website, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing inquiries@bitaxis.in.

1. Who we are

Bit Axis Software Solutions builds mobile apps, websites and business automations. We are based in India, at 104 Ghansoli, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India, and we are responsible (the “data controller”) for the personal information described in this policy.

Contact for anything privacy-related: inquiries@bitaxis.in

2. What we collect

When you fill in the contact form on bitaxis.in

  • Your name, email address and phone number (including country code)
  • Your business name
  • What you need help with, and the message you write to us

When you fill in one of our lead forms on Facebook or Instagram

  • Your name, email address and phone number (Meta may pre-fill these from your profile — you can edit them before sending)
  • Your answers to the questions in the form, such as your type of business and what you need help with
  • Basic campaign details from Meta, such as which ad and form you responded to and the date and time

When you email or call us

Your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us in that conversation.

We don’t ask for sensitive information (such as health, financial or identity documents) at the enquiry stage. Please don’t include it in your messages.

4. Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, under contracts that require them to protect it and use it only on our instructions:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram): if you use one of our lead forms, Meta collects the form on our behalf and passes it to us. Meta’s own use of your data is covered by Meta’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script and Google Fonts): to store our list of enquiries and to load the fonts used on this website.
  • n8n, hosted on Railway: the automation tool that moves a new enquiry into our records and sends your confirmation email.
  • Brevo: the service that delivers our automatic emails.
  • GoDaddy / Microsoft 365: our business email provider, where your replies to us are received.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

5. International transfers

Our team is based in India, so we access and work with your information from India. Some of our providers also store or process data outside Ireland, the European Economic Area or New Zealand, for example in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, and on providers that offer protections comparable to those in your country.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t become projects: deleted within 24 months of our last contact with you.
  • Clients: kept for as long as we work together, then for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting records.
  • You can ask us to delete your details sooner at any time (see below).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it if it’s wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete it;
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use it;
  • ask for it in a portable format; and
  • withdraw any consent you’ve given, at any time.

To use any of these rights, email inquiries@bitaxis.in. We’ll respond within one month (or within 20 working days if you are in New Zealand). We may need to confirm your identity first.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You can also complain to your local regulator:

  • Ireland: Data Protection Commission, dataprotection.ie
  • New Zealand: Office of the Privacy Commissioner, privacy.org.nz
  • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner’s Office, ico.org.uk
  • Elsewhere in the EU: the data protection authority in your country

8. Cookies and tracking on this website

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not include tracking pixels. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we’ll update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

9. Security

We use reputable providers, secure (HTTPS) connections, access controls and strong passwords to protect your information. No system is completely secure, but we limit access to your details to the people who need them to respond to you.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
